I very much liked this coffee. Great filter coffee, light fresh and herbal, and a surprisingly good espresso (intense and the minty notes 100% come out.)

## From the Roaster
> Yeisson Fabian produced this delicate Geisha lot at Finca El Vergel in Acevedo, facilitated by the Sudus Coffee Project. A pristine washed process unlocks crisp natural sugars, yielding a bright, sparkling cup with a refreshing mint finish.
