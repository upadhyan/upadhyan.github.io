This is a super clean, bright coffee. No insane complexity, but just very pleasant. 

## From the Roaster

> The Boyoca Community Blend is a unique lot that represents the work of smallholder campesinos farming the high slopes of Boyacá, a region emerging as a new frontier for Colombian specialty coffee. Producers here farm between 1,200 and 2,000 meters above sea level (masl), on steep terrain with cool climates and fertile soils.

> By pooling their harvests through community collaboration, they gain access to specialty markets and shared processing infrastructure. Washed and sun-dried, the Boyacá Community Blend offers lemon-tea brightness, candied orange sweetness, molasses richness, and a soft vanilla finish—a snapshot of both the region’s promise and its producers’ dedication.
